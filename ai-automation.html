The Rules Before the Tools

Permitted (approved platforms only)

  • Drafting control implementation narratives from sanitized inputs
  • Summarizing policy documents and STIG content for study
  • Generating training scenarios, quiz items, and awareness material
  • Script drafting for evidence collection (human review before any execution)
  • Meeting-note structuring and AAR first drafts

Prohibited — No Exceptions

  • CUI, vulnerability details, or system-identifying data into any unapproved AI tool
  • AI-generated artifacts submitted without documented human review — the signer owns every word
  • AI output as closure evidence or assessment results
  • Autonomous execution against production systems
  • Personal/free-tier AI accounts for any work product
The accountability ruleIf your signature block is on it, you reviewed it line by line. "The AI wrote it" has never survived a quality review, an inspection, or an incident investigation — and it never will.

Vetted Prompt Library

Prompts That Produce Reviewable Drafts

Tested against the Gold Standard rubric. The full, searchable library is hosted in the Cybersecurity AI Prompt Library ⤤. Replace bracketed placeholders with sanitized content only.

PL-01

Control Narrative Draft

"Draft an implementation statement for [control ID] describing [generic mechanism]. State what is implemented, how it's configured, who manages it, and how it's verified. Present tense, no aspirational language."

PL-02

Policy Plain-Language Summary

"Summarize this policy section for a new ISSO: required actions, deadlines, and escalation triggers as a checklist. Flag any ambiguity instead of resolving it yourself."

PL-03

POA&M Milestone Decomposition

"Break this remediation goal: [sanitized weakness type] into 3–5 verifiable milestones with measurable completion criteria for each. No milestone may be 'ongoing.'"

PL-04

Tabletop Inject Generator

"Generate 6 escalating injects for a [scenario type] tabletop testing [decision points]. Each inject names who receives it and what decision it forces."

PL-05

AAR First Draft

"Structure these sanitized timeline notes into AAR format: chronology, what worked, what failed, corrective actions with owner and date placeholders."

PL-06

NotebookLM Study Pack

Approved parameters: upload published policies from this site only; generate study guides and FAQs per document set; verify every citation against the source before sharing.
