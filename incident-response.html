First-Hour Discipline

Triage & Evidence Preservation

Initial Triage Checklist

  • Confirm reporter identity, system name, and physical/network location
  • Assign category and start the escalation clock
  • Isolate at the network layer if safe — do not power off or reboot
  • Identify data types in scope (CUI? PII? mission data?)
  • Open the incident log: timestamped, factual, no speculation
  • Establish the containment boundary with the IR Lead before touching the host

Evidence Preservation Guide

  • Volatile memory first: capture RAM before any other action if tooling is staged
  • Logs: export host, application, and network logs to write-once media; note retention rollover windows
  • Disk images: forensic image before remediation on any CAT-reportable incident
  • Chain of custody: who handled what, when — on the standard custody form, every transfer
  • Screenshots/photos: acceptable for on-screen ephemeral evidence; include system clock in frame
Containment BoundariesISSOs may isolate a single host. Segment-level isolation requires the IR Lead. Enclave or mission-system shutdown requires CISO approval with system-owner consultation — mission impact is weighed in minutes, not days.
