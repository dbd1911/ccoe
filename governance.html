Governance Framework

Who Decides What

Oversight Authority Model

CISO sets policy, owns portfolio risk posture, chairs the Cyber Governance Board, and is the escalation terminus for all cyber disputes.

AODR exercises delegated authorization review: validates package completeness, frames the risk recommendation, and routes to the AO. The AODR recommends; only the AO accepts risk and signs authorization decisions.

AO interaction model: the AO engages at three formal gates — strategy approval (Prepare), CSRR, and the decision briefing. Out-of-cycle engagement goes through the AODR.

Cyber Governance Board (CGB)

  • Charter: adjudicates significant changes, risk acceptances above ISSM threshold, policy waivers, and cross-portfolio mandates.
  • Cadence: second Thursday monthly; emergency session within 48h on CISO call.
  • Quorum: CISO (chair), AODR, senior ISSM, engineering lead, plus the affected system owner.
  • Escalation path: ISSO → ISSM → AODR → CGB → CISO → AO. Skipping levels is permitted only for active incidents.
