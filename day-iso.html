System Owner (ISO)

Every Army system has an appointed ISO — and in this portfolio that appointment is usually carried by the Program Manager or the Product/Project Director. You own the system end to end: procured, developed, integrated, modified, operated, maintained, and disposed. The organization’s single ISSM and your ISSO(s) execute cybersecurity for you; you fund it, enable it, and answer to the AO for the posture.

What Is My Job

The Charter — What the Army Expects of a System Owner (ISO)

Split the way you'll actually use it: what you own outright, what you support, what you hand upward. When ownership is unclear, this page is the tiebreaker.

You Own
  • The operational security posture of your system(s) — accountable to the AO chain
  • Appointing the cyber cadre: the organization’s single ISSM and your ISSO(s) — in writing (former PM-level ISSM billets are now ISSOs)
  • Funding remediation: POA&Ms resourced to close inside SLA timelines
You Support
  • Your ISSO — your key cyber personnel: keep them in every loop (design changes, deliverables, fielding)
  • Your ISSM — the organization’s one security manager, your governance chain to the AO
  • The ISSE integration — you ensure every program has one, per the reg
You Escalate
  • Posture you cannot fund or resource
  • Authorization decisions — the AO owns them; you enforce them

The Chain

You report to: PEO (acquisition side) / the AO chain (posture side)
Who works with you: the ISSM (one for the organization) + your ISSOs (former PM-level ISSM billets) + the assigned ISSE

Army Line

DA PAM 25-2-14 §3-10(a): every DoD IS and PIT system MUST have an appointed ISO or PM/SM, responsible for procurement through disposal — for ensuring the appropriate operational security posture — appointing the ISSM/ISSO structure, categorizing information types with the cadre, reporting status to the AO, and keeping every program’s ISSE fully integrated into systems engineering. The ISO and the PM/SM are the same accountability; in this portfolio the badge usually reads PM or Product Director. Org model: one ISSM, ISSOs at the program level (formerly ISSM billets).
