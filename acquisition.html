9 clauses shown

SOW-CY-01

RMF Support Baseline

Contractor shall execute RMF activities under government direction, producing artifacts compliant with CCoE templates and accepted only upon government QA review.

SOW-CY-02

Secure Coding Requirements

All delivered code shall pass static analysis with zero unmitigated critical/high findings and comply with applicable STIG/SRG configuration baselines.

CUI-CL-01

CUI Safeguarding

Contractor systems processing CUI shall meet NIST SP 800-171 requirements; flow-down to subcontractors is mandatory and verifiable on request.

CUI-CL-02

Incident Notification SLA

Contractor shall report suspected cyber incidents affecting CUI or government systems within 4 hours of discovery via the government contact tree.

DEL-CY-01

RMF Deliverables List

Required with delivery: SBOM (SPDX or CycloneDX), draft SSP inputs, hardware/software inventory, authenticated scan results ≤30 days old, and STIG checklists.

DEL-CY-02

Acceptance Criteria

Deliverables are accepted only after government QA against the published checklist; one funded correction cycle, after which corrections are at contractor expense.

CLD-CY-01

FedRAMP / IL Residency

Cloud services shall hold FedRAMP authorization at the IL level matching data categorization (IL4/IL5 as specified); data residency restricted to approved regions.

PER-CY-01

Personnel Access Standards

Contractor personnel accessing government systems shall meet position-designated investigation levels and complete CUI and incident-reporting training before access.

PER-CY-02

Departure Notification

Contractor shall notify the COR within 1 business day of personnel departure; account deactivation is verified by the ISSO within 24 hours of notice.

Contract Deliverable Detail

What "Done" Means at Delivery

Minimum Cyber Deliverable Set

  • SBOM — machine-readable (SPDX/CycloneDX), regenerated at every release
  • SSP inputs — implementation statements for contractor-managed controls, in CCoE template format
  • Boundary & data flow diagrams — current as-built, not as-proposed
  • Authenticated scans — ACAS-compatible output, ≤30 days old at delivery
  • STIG checklists — .ckl format with justification for every open finding
  • HW/SW inventory — reconcilable against the SBOM and scans

Cybersecurity Cost Estimating Guide

Rule-of-thumb planning factors so cyber work is funded, not absorbed:

  • Initial authorization support: 8–12% of development cost for a moderate system
  • Continuous monitoring: 2–4% annually of system sustainment cost
  • Significant change re-assessment: budget per event, not from sustainment
  • Standalone / closed networks: add 15–25% for manual evidence overhead
