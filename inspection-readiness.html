Master Readiness Checklist

The Standard, System by System

Documentation Readiness

  • SSP current within 90 days, matching the as-built system
  • Authorization letter, SAR, and RAW on file and consistent with eMASS
  • POA&Ms current — no missing milestones, no stale dates
  • ConMon evidence for the last 12 months retrievable in under 10 minutes
  • Change records reconciled against the configuration baseline
  • All controlled documents carry complete metadata (owner/version/dates/approval)

Operational Readiness

  • Scan cadence proof: last 8 weekly ACAS cycles on file
  • STIG checklists current against the tracked baseline versions
  • Account recertification records inside their windows
  • Incident reporting drill completed within the last 12 months
  • Personnel 8140 qualification status current in the tracker
  • Physical/media controls validated for standalone segments
Artifact Binder Structure

Eight Tabs, Every System, Same Order

Inspectors see hundreds of binders. A predictable structure signals discipline before they read a single page.

Tab 1 — Authorization

Decision letter, conditions, SAR/RAW, authorization boundary statement.

Tab 2 — SSP & Diagrams

Current SSP, boundary and data-flow diagrams, HW/SW inventory.

Tab 3 — POA&M

Active items, closure evidence samples, exception approvals.

Tab 4 — ConMon

Scan summaries, STIG status, annual review records.

Tab 5 — Change Mgmt

Significant change requests, minor memos, impact analyses.

Tab 6 — Incidents

Reports, AARs, tabletop results for the current cycle.

Tab 7 — Personnel

Role assignments, 8140 status, access recertification proof.

Tab 8 — Decisions

Risk acceptances, waivers, and governance records affecting this system.

Mock Inspections & Interview Prep

Mock Inspection Methodology

  • Scope: two systems per cycle, selected by risk and expiration proximity
  • Format: 2-day no-notice document pull + 1 day of staff interviews, scored against the real inspection rubric
  • Output: findings memo with 30-day fix suspense; re-check before the binder deadline
  • Next cycle: 03–14 AUG 2026 — schedule posted on the Assessment Calendar

Assessor Interview Preparation

Inspectors test whether people understand what they signed. Practice answering, out loud:

  • "Walk me through your system boundary."
  • "How do you know your controls are still working today?"
  • "Show me how this POA&M closure was verified."
  • "What happens, step by step, if you suspect a data spill right now?"
  • "Who can accept risk on this system — and who cannot?"
