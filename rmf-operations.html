Common Package Mistakes

The reject-pile patterns: vague narratives, stale scans, boundary drift, orphan POA&Ms.
Acceptable Narrative

AC-2(3): The system automatically disables user accounts after 35 days of inactivity via Active Directory GPO "ACME-AC-2-3-Disable" (screenshot, artifact AC-2.3-01). The ISSO reviews the disabled-accounts report monthly (sample report, artifact AC-2.3-02). Exceptions require a signed waiver from the ISSM (waiver log, artifact AC-2.3-03).

States what, how, who, how often, and names the evidence.

Unacceptable Narrative

AC-2(3): The organization disables inactive accounts in accordance with policy. This control is implemented and compliant.

Restates the control, names no mechanism, no frequency, no role, no evidence. Automatic quality-review reject.

Dedicated Pathway Guides

Authorization Pathways

Ten distinct compliance paths. Filter by your system's situation; each guide covers entry criteria, required artifacts, duration limits, and renewal triggers.

10 items shown

Standard

Full ATO

Complete RMF execution with a standard 3-year authorization term. The default path for enduring systems with stable boundaries. Requires full assessment, complete decision package, and a continuous monitoring plan accepted by the AO.

Temporary

IATT — Interim Authorization to Test

Time-boxed authority for test activity only, with explicit temporary boundaries and operational restrictions. No live operational data unless specifically authorized. Hard expiration — no auto-renewal.

Conditional

Conditional ATO

Authorization granted with binding conditions: named POA&M milestones, restricted functionality, or mandated compensating controls. Missing a condition milestone triggers AODR escalation and potential revocation.

Connection

ATC Only

Authority to Connect for systems whose authorization is owned elsewhere but which require a connection decision on this network. Connection-oriented evidence: boundary, data flows, and ports/protocols/services.

Limited Scope

Assess-Only

For components and applications inheriting their hosting environment's authorization. Assessment evidence is produced and registered, but no separate authorization decision is issued.

Special

Standalone Systems

Zero-to-limited scan pathways for disconnected systems. Manual STIG review and physical/administrative control emphasis replace network scanning cadence. See the Standalone SOP for evidence substitutions.

Special

Closed-Restricted Networks

Networks with controlled interfaces and restricted membership. Audit emphasis on boundary protection, media control, and cross-domain touchpoints. Annual closed-network audit required.

Continuous

Agile ATO / Continuous Authorization

DevSecOps-aligned cycle: authorize the pipeline and platform once, then sustain authorization through automated control evidence, gated releases, and continuous monitoring dashboards.

Extension

ATO Extension

Short, justified extension of an expiring authorization. Requires current scan posture, POA&M health review, and a credible plan to complete reauthorization. Extensions are exceptions, not strategy.

Reciprocity

Reciprocity

Accepting another DoD organization's authorization body of evidence. Execution terms: complete BoE transfer, delta assessment of local risk, and a documented acceptance decision — never rubber-stamp.
