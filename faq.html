Frequently Asked Questions

14 questions shown

RMF

How long does an initial ATO take?

Plan 6–9 months from categorization to decision for a moderate system with a prepared team. The biggest variable is artifact quality — packages built from Gold Standard templates clear assessment roughly 40% faster.

RMF

Which authorization pathway applies to my system?

Start with the pathway cards on the RMF Operations Hub. If two seem to fit, your ISSM makes the call during strategy approval — don't self-select a temporary pathway to dodge full ATO work.

eMASS

How do I get an eMASS account?

Request via Tools & Dashboards → eMASS. You need: CAC, completed eMASS computer-based training certificate, and ISSM sponsorship naming your system roles. Provisioning runs ~5 business days.

eMASS

This site's data disagrees with eMASS. Which wins?

eMASS, always. Dashboards here refresh nightly and exist for visibility, not record-keeping. If they disagree for more than 48 hours, report it to the Knowledge Manager.

POA&M

What closure evidence actually passes review?

Proof the fix exists and proof it works: config export or patch record plus a clean rescan or manual verification dated after the fix. Screenshots without timestamps and "completed per admin" notes are auto-rejected.

POA&M

Can I get an SLA extension?

Only via a written exception request with compensating measures, endorsed by your ISSM. KEV-listed items have no extension path — escalate resourcing instead.

Change

Minor change or significant change?

Apply the rubric in the Change Management Policy: anything altering the boundary, data types, user population, external connections, or security architecture is significant. When genuinely unsure, file it as significant — downgrades are painless, upgrades mid-stream are not.

Standalone

My system can't be scanned. Now what?

The Standalone SOP defines substitute evidence: manual STIG checklists, signed configuration attestations, and quarterly physical audits. "Can't scan" never means "no evidence" — it means different evidence.

Cloud

The CSP is FedRAMP authorized. Am I done?

No — you inherit the provider's controls, not your own. Customer-responsibility controls (IAM config, data handling, logging integration) remain yours and are assessed normally. The inheritance matrix in eMASS shows the split.

CUI

Can I email CUI?

Only encrypted (signed and encrypted via approved PKI), only to recipients with lawful government purpose, with the banner marking in the body and "CUI" in the subject line. Personal accounts and unmanaged mobile: never.

CUI

How do I mark a CUI document?

Banner top and bottom ("CUI"), designation indicator block on page one (owner, category, distribution, POC), and portion markings where required by category. The CUI Handling SOP has worked examples.

Contractor

A contractor deliverable failed cyber QA. Who fixes it?

The contractor, under the acceptance-criteria clause — one funded correction cycle, then corrections at contractor expense. Route findings through the COR, never directly to contractor staff.

Incident

What if I report something and it's nothing?

Good. A benign report costs 20 minutes; an unreported incident costs an authorization. No one is penalized for good-faith reporting — this is written policy, not a slogan.

Incident

I clicked a phishing link. What now?

Call the watch desk immediately (treat as malware timeline), leave the machine on but disconnect from the network, and change credentials from a different, known-good device. Speed matters more than embarrassment.

CCIControl Correlation Identifier — decomposes NIST controls into single, assessable statements used in STIGs and eMASS.
CUIControlled Unclassified Information — unclassified information requiring safeguarding under law, regulation, or government-wide policy.

IATTInterim Authorization to Test — short-duration authorization for testing in an operational environment, with explicit boundaries and restrictions.

ISSMInformation System Security Manager — manages cybersecurity for assigned systems/programs; the ISSO's governance chain.

SARSecurity Assessment Report — the assessor's findings on control effectiveness, feeding the RAW and AO decision.

SCA-RSCA Representative — performs assessment activities under SCA authority and direction.
