AO Risk Decision Framework

Four Factors Behind Every Decision

The AODR frames every authorization recommendation against these factors. Package narratives that address all four explicitly clear review fastest.

Factor 1

System Criticality

Mission dependency tier: what stops if this system stops? Direct fires support weighs differently than a training scheduler.

Factor 2

Data Categorization

FIPS 199 levels plus CUI categories carried. Aggregation matters — many low-sensitivity records can be a high-sensitivity holding.

Factor 3

Connectivity Profile

Internet-facing, enclave-internal, cross-domain, or air-gapped. Exposure surface scales likelihood across every finding.

Factor 4

Compensating Posture

What stands between the weakness and the adversary today — segmentation, monitoring depth, response speed.

Authorization Priority Score

Priority = (Criticality × 0.4) + (Expiration urgency × 0.3) + (Risk severity × 0.2) + (Mission tempo × 0.1)

Standardized 1–10 scoring per factor ranks the assessment queue. The score is published with the queue — no system jumps the line without a recorded CISO decision.

Risk Acceptance & Mission Impact

Translate CVEs into Mission Language

The AO accepts mission risk, not CVSS scores. Every risk acceptance memo must complete this translation chain:

1

Technical Finding

"CVE-2026-31822, remote code execution on the LOGNET-04 web tier, CVSS 9.8, KEV-listed."

2

System Effect

"Successful exploitation grants adversary control of the logistics request broker."

3

Mission Degradation

"Supply requisitions for three supported brigades revert to manual processing — 72-hour fulfillment becomes 7–10 days."

4

Decision Frame

"Accept for 30 days with WAF rule and enhanced logging, or take a 4-hour outage now to patch." The AO chooses between mission costs, stated plainly.

Reporting Instruments

Registers, Top Risks & Quad Charts

Portfolio Risk Register Template

Standard fields: risk statement, owner, severity, residual, treatment, review date. One register per portfolio segment, reviewed monthly.
Top Portfolio Systemic Risks

Dynamic top-10 list maintained by the Risk Lead — currently led by assessor saturation, legacy OT firmware, and cloud control inheritance gaps.
Executive Quad Chart

One slide: risk posture / drivers / decisions needed / trend. The only risk format leadership sees — anything longer gets summarized into it anyway.
