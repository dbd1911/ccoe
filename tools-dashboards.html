The Tools You'll Actually Use

System of Record

eMASS

Purpose: Enterprise Mission Assurance Support Service — authoritative record for packages, controls, and POA&Ms.

  • Owner: AODR office (org administrator)
  • Access: CAC + eMASS CBT certificate + ISSM sponsorship form → ~5 business days
  • Guidance: Managing eMASS & ATO Workflows (IC-204) in the Training Academy; quick-reference cards in Templates
  • Support: emass.support@demo.mil · office hours Tuesdays 1000
Scanning

ACAS (Tenable)

Purpose: Enterprise vulnerability scanning and reporting.

  • Owner: Vulnerability Management Cell
  • Access: read access for ISSOs by default; operator roles require ACAS course completion
  • Cadence: weekly authenticated scans, per the vuln standards; newest detection plugins tracked on the Threat Intel feeds (tenable.com/plugins ⤤)
  • Support: vuln.cell@demo.mil
Compliance

SCC — SCAP Compliance Checker

Purpose: NIWC-built SCAP 1.3 validated scanner — automated STIG benchmark scans producing results importable to .ckl checklists.

  • Owner: Engineering (version tracking)
  • Access: free public download — no CAC required for the tool itself
  • Get it: DoD Cyber Exchange — SCAP tools ⤤
  • Guidance: pair with the STIG Execution Handbook on the Engineering Center
Compliance

STIG Viewer

Purpose: Review STIG content, manage .ckl checklists, document finding status.

  • Owner: Engineering (version tracking)
  • Access: approved software list — self-service install; public download at DoD Cyber Exchange ⤤
  • Guidance: STIG Execution Handbook on the Engineering Center; current STIG releases at public.cyber.mil/stigs/downloads ⤤
Analytics

Power BI

Purpose: Portfolio dashboards — ATO health, POA&M aging, queue metrics.

  • Owner: Metrics & Performance Center
  • Access: view access by role; report-builder access by request with supervisor endorsement
  • Support: metrics@demo.mil
Collaboration

SharePoint & Teams

Access Provisioning

Standard Access Workflow

1

Prerequisites

CAC issued, baseline training current (CUI + incident reporting), tool-specific CBT where required.

2

Sponsorship

ISSM (or supervisor for non-system tools) endorses the request naming exact roles and systems.

3

Provisioning

Tool owner provisions least-privilege roles; SLA 5 business days, expedite via the watch desk for incident needs.

4

Recertification

Privileged access recertified every 90 days; departure triggers same-day deactivation per the contractor and personnel standards.
