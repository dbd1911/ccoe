eMASS

Purpose: Enterprise Mission Assurance Support Service — authoritative record for packages, controls, and POA&Ms.

  • Owner: AODR office (org administrator)
  • Access: CAC + eMASS CBT certificate + ISSM sponsorship form → ~5 business days
  • Guidance: Managing eMASS & ATO Workflows (IC-204) in the Training Academy; quick-reference cards in Templates
  • Support: usarmy.orlando.cpe-st3.mbx.cybersecurity-office@army.mil · office hours Tuesdays 1000
Scanning

ACAS (Tenable)

Purpose: Enterprise vulnerability scanning and reporting.

  • Owner: Vulnerability Management Cell
  • Access: read access for ISSOs by default; operator roles require ACAS course completion
  • Cadence: weekly authenticated scans, per the vuln standards; newest detection plugins tracked on the Threat Intel feeds (tenable.com/plugins ⤤)
  • Support: usarmy.orlando.cpe-st3.mbx.cybersecurity-office@army.mil

Access Provisioning

Standard Access Workflow

1

Prerequisites

CAC issued, baseline training current (CUI + incident reporting), tool-specific CBT where required.

2

Sponsorship

ISSM (or supervisor for non-system tools) endorses the request naming exact roles and systems.

3

Provisioning

Tool owner provisions least-privilege roles; SLA 5 business days, expedite via the incident inbox (usarmy.orlando.cpe-st3.mbx.cybersecurity-office@army.mil) for incident needs.

4

Recertification

Privileged access recertified every 90 days; departure triggers same-day deactivation per the contractor and personnel standards.
