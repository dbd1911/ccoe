Everyone, Regardless of Role

True on Day One for All of Us

Role specifics live on the role pages. These are the shared moves every new arrival in this portfolio makes.

Get access — in the right order

SLAs stack: eMASS provisioning is 5 business days, so it goes in day 1 with the ISSM’s sponsorship. ACAS/SCC next (~3 days). Badge + network + Teams same-day from your sponsor. The full access workflow lists every tool and approver.

Meet your counterpart — day 2

This portfolio runs on relationships: ISSO↔system owner, ISSO↔ISSM (one for the organization), ISSO↔CHEN/ISSE, system owner↔AO chain. Your role page names exactly who yours are. Fifteen minutes now saves weeks of routing later.

Learn the one rule

If it was not recorded, it was not decided. Every authority here — AO, ISSM, CHEN, PM — works the same way: decisions land in writing (eMASS, ARB minutes, CGB records, POA&M). Day one, learn where the records live for YOUR role.

The landscape in 60 seconds

Governance flow: CISO → AODR → AO sign the risk decisions; the single ISSM + the ISSO cadre (former PM-level ISSM billets) prepare everything they sign. Program flow: PEO/PM resource the work; CHEN/LPE + product teams build it; the ISSE welds security into the design. Full working-relationship map →

First 60 days, any role

Days 1–10: access in order + read-first list (your role page has both). Days 11–30: shadow the rhythm — sit in your chain's reviews before you own one. Days 31–60: own your first deliverable end to end, with your counterpart reviewing.

The habits that make new arrivals successful here: ask early, record everything, and never let a clock surprise you — the SLA timers on the Vulnerability & POA&M page run whether you watch them or not.
