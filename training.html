Three Proficiency Tiers

Thirty courses, three tiers. Each course runs fifteen modules with a required knowledge check per module — you answer to proceed, and after a second miss the correct answer is explained so you can continue — then a comprehensive forty-item capstone at eighty percent to pass. Clear the capstone to earn the course certificate.
