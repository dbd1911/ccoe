Mistake PatternWhy It FailsThe Fix
Future-tense narratives ("will be configured")Describes intent, not implementation — unassessablePresent tense, named mechanism, named verifier
Copy-pasted control text as the "implementation"Restates the requirement instead of answering itSay what this system does, specifically
Undated or unscoped screenshots as evidenceUnverifiable — could be any system, any timeTimestamp, hostname, and collection note on every artifact
"Ongoing" as a POA&M milestoneNo completion criterion — can never be verified closedDecompose into dated, measurable milestones
Boundary diagram from the design proposalInspectors compare against as-built and find deltasRe-validate the diagram after every change window
