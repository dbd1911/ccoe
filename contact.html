How It Works

  • Submit any question — policy, priorities, "why do we do it this way" — through the box below. Anonymous submissions accepted.
  • Every question gets an answer: live at the monthly session (last Friday, 1100) or in writing within two weeks.
  • Answers of general interest are published to the FAQ and the weekly digest.
  • Disagreement is welcome; this channel exists precisely for the questions people hesitate to ask in formation.
