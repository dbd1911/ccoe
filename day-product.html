The Charter — What the Army Expects of a Product Manager / Owner

Split the way you'll actually use it: what you own outright, what you support, what you hand upward. When ownership is unclear, this page is the tiebreaker.

You Own
  • The backlog's Secure-by-Design items — auth, logging, crypto defaults as stories
  • Release risk posture per increment — what ships, and what is known about it
  • User-facing security behavior
You Support
  • The CHEN's architecture discipline
  • The pipeline’s attestations (LPE-provided)
You Escalate
  • Story-level security debt that compounds
  • Pipeline control gaps
