What Is My Job

The Charter — What the Army Expects of a Lead Project Engineer

Split the way you'll actually use it: what you own outright, what you support, what you hand upward. When ownership is unclear, this page is the tiebreaker.

You Own
  • The integration evidence pipeline — scan artifacts, attestations, test results, clean and on time
  • Cross-team technical coordination — contractor lines ↔ government lines
  • Risk-tracked integration decisions — nothing silently baked in
You Support
  • CHEN baseline + ARB outcomes
  • Product teams' increments
You Escalate
  • Evidence gaps discovered late
  • Contractor artifacts repeatedly missing acceptance
