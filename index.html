Mission Statement

Enable the mission. Own the risk.

The CPEST3 Cybersecurity Center of Excellence drives disciplined Risk Management Framework execution across the Army cyber portfolio, establishes explicit risk ownership at every echelon, builds workforce capability, and delivers the real-time portfolio health metrics leadership needs to make evidence-based decisions. Compliance is the floor — risk-aware mission enablement is the standard.

CISO Standing Message
