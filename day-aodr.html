What Is My Job

The Charter — What the Army Expects of a AO & AODR

Split the way you'll actually use it: what you own outright, what you support, what you hand upward. When ownership is unclear, this page is the tiebreaker.

You Own
  • The authorization decision itself — AO, non-delegable, ever
  • Delegated review / coordination / recommendation — AODR
  • A portfolio health view with nothing surprising in it
You Support
  • The CGB — both roles feed it
  • Programs — decision-ready briefs
You Escalate
  • Threat-informed operational concerns → the Army Strategy Board lane (PPM CIO-065)
  • Actions exceeding your delegation

The Chain

You report to: the service CIO / acquisition chain
Who works with you: your AODR office + the ISSM cadre

Army Line

DA PAM 25-2-14 §3-5 is explicit: the only thing an AO cannot delegate is the authorization decision and its signature. Everything else is AODR territory; PPM CIO-065 adds the DoDIN-A/NAO alignment + ASB escalation lane.
