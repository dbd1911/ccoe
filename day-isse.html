Day One · SECURE DESIGN · SP 800-160

ISSE

You engineer security into systems as they're built, changed, and verified — the technical bridge between the program's intent and the control set's reality.

What Is My Job?

YOU OWN
  • Control implementation + inheritance analysis (inherited/hybrid/new, per boundary)
  • Boundary + data-flow architecture accuracy (what the SSP swears by)
  • STIG-ability reviews: every design scannable, patchable, evidenceable
YOU SUPPORT
  • The Chief Engineer’s baseline
  • ISSO implementations (you design, they execute)
YOU ESCALATE
  • Designs that cannot meet the control set (say it early — not at the ARB)
  • Inheritance assumptions that cannot be evidenced

The Chain

You report to: Chief Engineer (program side) / ISSM (portfolio side)
Who works with you: Developer/621 engineers

Army Line (the regulation)

The ISSE is a named member of the Army RMF team in DA PAM 25-2-14 §3-1(b)(10). Your work product is the SSP’s engineering backbone.
