What Is My Job

The Charter — What the Army Expects of a ISSE

Split the way you'll actually use it: what you own outright, what you support, what you hand upward. When ownership is unclear, this page is the tiebreaker.

You Own
  • Control implementation + inheritance analysis — inherited/hybrid/new, per boundary
  • Boundary + data-flow architecture accuracy — what the SSP swears by
  • STIG-ability reviews — every design scanable, patchable, evidenceable
You Support
  • The Chief Engineer's baseline
  • ISSO implementations — you design, they execute
You Escalate
  • Designs that cannot meet the control set — say it early, not at the ARB
  • Inheritance assumptions that cannot be evidenced

The Chain

You report to: Chief Engineer (program side) / ISSM (portfolio side)
Who works with you: developer engineers + the ISSO

Army Line

The ISSE is a named member of the Army RMF team under DA PAM 25-2-14 §3-1(b)(10) — your work product is the SSP’s engineering backbone.
