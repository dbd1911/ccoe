What Is My Job

The Charter — What the Army Expects of a Chief Engineer

Split the way you'll actually use it: what you own outright, what you support, what you hand upward. When ownership is unclear, this page is the tiebreaker.

You Own
  • The technical baseline + its change discipline — nobody re-architects on a whim
  • Architecture review discipline — ARB engagement at every gate
  • A ranked, visible technical-risk register — not folklore
You Support
  • The ISSE cadre — your day-to-day engineering arm
  • LPE integration evidence
You Escalate
  • Architecture decisions that cannot satisfy control inheritance
  • Gate readiness you cannot certify

The Chain

You report to: PM
Who works with you: ISSEs + contractor engineering leads

Army Line

Your authority flows from PM delegation; your discipline is SP 800-160 practice. DCWF 641 covers the systems-requirements and architecture spine of the role.
