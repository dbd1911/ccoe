What Is My Job

The Charter — What the Army Expects of a ISSO

Split the way you'll actually use it: what you own outright, what you support, what you hand upward. When ownership is unclear, this page is the tiebreaker.

You Own
  • Continuous monitoring of assigned systems: scans, artifacts, ConMon evidence
  • Implementation statements and evidence that survive quality review
  • POA&M hygiene: honest entries, tracked milestones, evidenced closures
You Support
  • Package assembly for the ISSM — your artifacts are its raw material
  • ISSE implementation reviews on your systems
You Escalate
  • Controls you cannot implement as designed — those become POA&M items, not shrugs
  • Suspected incidents: immediately, per the call order

The Chain

You report to: ISSM (DCWF 722)
Who works with you: the ISSE on your systems + the SA team

Army Line

DA PAM 25-2-14 §3-15 spells out ISSO duties verbatim — coordination, posture maintenance, protective-action initiation, access screening, enforcement. Your job description is literally in the pamphlet; this page is how to live it.
