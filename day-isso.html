Day One · CYBER OPERATIONS · DCWF 721

ISSO

You keep assigned systems' security posture healthy day to day — the hands-on executor of the RMF. The ISSM sets the strategy; you live it.

What Is My Job?

YOU OWN
  • Continuous monitoring of assigned systems: scans, artifacts, ConMon evidence
  • Implementation statements + evidence that survive quality review
  • POA&M hygiene: honest entries, tracked milestones, evidenced closures
YOU SUPPORT
  • Package assembly for the ISSM (your artifacts are the raw material)
  • ISSE implementation reviews
YOU ESCALATE
  • Controls you cannot implement as designed (become POA&M items, not shrugs)
  • Suspected incidents — immediately, per the call order

The Chain

You report to: ISSM
Who works with you: The system’s ISSE + SA team

Army Line (the regulation)

DA PAM 25-2-14 §3-15 spells out your duties verbatim — coordination, posture maintenance, protective actions, access screening, enforcement. Your job description is literally in the pamphlet; this page is how to live it.
