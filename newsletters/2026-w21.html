Gold Standard Mandate — Results

One quarter after gold-standard examples shipped alongside every template, the package rejection rate is down 12 points. The pattern is clear: teams that compare drafts against the gold standard before submission pass quality review the first time.

What Changed

Every template row in the library now pairs the blank file with a completed, sanitized example that passed review. If your artifact type is missing a gold standard, request one through the AI & Automation intake.
