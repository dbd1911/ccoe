Day One · PROGRAM MANAGEMENT · DCWF 801

PM & APM

You own the program: cost, schedule, performance — and its cybersecurity posture. Cyber works for your mission; you resource it.

What Is My Job?

YOU OWN
  • Cyber requirements getting INTO the contract (clause library → SOW)
  • Cybersecurity as a funded line item, not a garnish
  • The program’s risk posture visible to you — never a gate-day surprise
YOU SUPPORT
  • ISSM/ISSO execution (you resource, they operate)
  • ISSE/CHEN design discipline
YOU ESCALATE
  • Funding gaps that would breach SLAs
  • Gate decisions you cannot evidence

The Chain

You report to: PEO
Who works with you: APM + CHEN + LPE + product managers + the cyber team (ISSM/ISSO/ISSE)

Army Line (the regulation)

DA PAM 25-2-14 §3-10 makes you (ISO/PM) accountable for the security posture of every component system — appointing the ISSM/ISSO, categorizing information types, funding remediation to timelines. It’s regulation, not best practice.
