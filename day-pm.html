What Is My Job

The Charter — What the Army Expects of a Program Manager & APM

Split the way you'll actually use it: what you own outright, what you support, what you hand upward. When ownership is unclear, this page is the tiebreaker.

You Own
  • Cyber requirements getting INTO the contract — clause library → SOW language
  • Cybersecurity as a funded line item, not a garnish
  • A risk posture you can see daily — never a gate-day surprise
You Support
  • ISSM/ISSO execution — you resource, they operate
  • ISSE/CHEN design discipline
You Escalate
  • Funding gaps that would breach SLAs
  • Gate decisions you cannot evidence

The Chain

You report to: PEO
Who works with you: APM + CHEN + LPE + the cyber team (ISSM/ISSO/ISSE)

Army Line

DA PAM 25-2-14 §3-10 makes you accountable for the security posture of every component system: appointing the ISSM/ISSO, categorizing information types, funding remediation to timelines. It is regulation, not best practice.
