## How to Navigate (30 seconds)

- **New to a role?** → **Day One** ([day-one.html](https://dbd1911.github.io/ccoe/day-one.html)) — pick your role tile; each role has its own page: what the job is, the first 10 days in order, the battle rhythm, and your official playbook.
- **"Who do I ask? / How do I…?"** → **FAQ & Knowledge Base** ([faq.html](https://dbd1911.github.io/ccoe/faq.html)) — 207 searchable answers + full-site index search.
- **"Where's the form?"** → **Templates** — every artifact in blank + gold-standard pairs.
- **"Is my system about to expire?"** → **Assessment Calendar** — the expiration radar + Outlook .ics.
- **The one rule everywhere:** *if it wasn't recorded, it wasn't decided* — decisions land in writing (eMASS, ARB minutes, CGB records, POA&M).
